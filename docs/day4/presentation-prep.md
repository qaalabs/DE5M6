# EPA Discussion Practice ~ Presentation Prep

## What you are doing

Prepare a **3-4 minute talk** on one topic from the list below.

This is practice for the EPA professional discussion — a structured conversation where you explain your knowledge and experience to an assessor. No slides. Just speak clearly.

- Your example can come from your workplace, from this module, or from something like the bikezelo activity today
- You can pick a full topic or a single KSB if you prefer to go narrow
- Be ready for follow-up questions from the group

---

## Professional Discussion Topics

### Data Quality and Performance

- Explains how they monitor different types of data store to optimise system management, performance and availability. *(K1, S7)*
- Defines data normalisation principles and the advantages they achieve for data protection, redundancy and inconsistent dependency. *(K3)*
- Explains the inherent risks of data and how to ensure data quality. *(K5)*
- Explains the principles of descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics. *(K27)*
- Describes how they use data ingestion frameworks such as streaming, batching and on demand services to move data from one location to another in order to optimise data ingestion processes. *(K18, S15)*
- [distinction] Compares and contrasts the different types of data stores they have used and how they optimised performance. *(K1, S7)*

### Problem Solving

- Describes technology and service management best practice. *(K22)*
- Explains how they identify and escalate risks and incidents, communicating downtime and issues with database access in line with policies in order to mitigate operational impact whilst ensuring business continuity. *(K21, S10, S18, S19)*
- Explains how they have maintained a working knowledge of data use cases within organisations. *(S12)*
- Explains how their analysis of root cause investigation is used to respond to incidents within data processing pipelines, whilst troubleshooting and providing resolutions to stakeholders. *(K23, S8, S20, B5)*
- [distinction] Justifies the approach taken to manage risks and incidents to maintain business continuity. *(S18, S19)*

### Regulatory Compliance

- Explains their use of data, information security standards, ethical practices and data management policies and procedures to ensure data systems are used securely and in accordance with relevant legislation. *(K11, S13)*
- Explains the legislative framework associated with the use and collation of data, including concepts of data governance and regulatory requirements. *(K10)*

### Continuous Improvement

- Outlines how they evaluate opportunities to extract value from existing data products whilst applying the principles of data and considering costs, environmental impact and potential operating benefits. *(K16, S11)*
- Explains how they take personal responsibility to identify new tools and technologies and recommend potential opportunities for use in their organisation, in order to prioritise environmental sustainability outcomes. *(S14, B4)*
- Explains how they take a quality focussed approach to identify and remediate technical debt and assess for updates and obsolescence, by utilising peer review techniques and capturing good practice. *(K28, S21, B3)*
- Explains how they apply ways of working that support software development principles and advocate software development best practice when working with other data professionals. *(S17)*
- Explains how they identify and assess new technologies, as well as gaps in existing tools and technologies, that offer increased performance of data products. *(S25, S28)*
- [distinction] Evaluates the impact that the implementation of identified new technologies would have on practices within the organisation. *(S25, S28)*

### Continuous Professional Development

- Explains how they have implemented personal strategies for keeping up to date with new ways of working and developments in emerging, contemporary and advanced technologies, in order to optimise sustainable products and services. *(K29, S29, B6)*
- [distinction] Evaluate the impact that keeping up to date with technological developments has had on their own professional development. *(S29)*

## How to prepare

1. Pick a topic — ideally one you have a real example for
2. Think of a specific example: a project, a task, a problem you solved
3. Structure it: what you did, why you did it, what happened
4. Prepare to answer: *"How did you decide that?"* or *"What would you do differently?"*
