## <mark>Workplace connections ~ discussion</mark>

**~20 mins — open discussion, draw on 2-3 people per question**

- How does your workplace handle incidents like this — is there a process, or is it ad hoc?
- Who gets notified when something breaks — is it clear, or does it depend on who's around?
- What would have made the fire drill easier if it reflected your real workplace?
- What's one thing from today you'd want to take back and try?
