## <mark>Technical Debt Audit ~ in breakout groups</mark>

- Part 1 (~30 min): audit all files using the checklist, record findings in the shared template - one row per issue
- Part 2 (~15 min): as a group, agree your single most significant issue + priority fix through your lens
- Prompt groups that go quiet in Part 1 - point them at the checklist section for their lens
