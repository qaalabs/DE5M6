## <mark>EPA bridge</mark>

The EPA includes a **professional discussion** — a structured conversation with an assessor about your knowledge and workplace experience.

This afternoon you will practice that.

- Pick a KSB from the list that connects to something real in your workplace
- Prepare a 3-4 minute talk — no slides, just speak
- The group will ask follow-up questions

*The goal is to get comfortable talking about your work in a structured way.*
