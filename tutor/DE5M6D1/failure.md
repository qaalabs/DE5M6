## <mark>Group discussion</mark> - What happened?

### Opening Question

- Think of a time when a system failure impacted you personally
- Could be at work, online shopping, banking, social media.

### Individual Reflection

- Give learners time to think of their example
- Encourage them to consider: What broke? How did they find out? What was the impact?

### Sharing Round

- Each learner shares their story (1-2 minutes each)

**Day 1 ~ outside looking in** ~ **Day 2 ~ inside looking out**

---

<mark>**Day 1**: A system failed and you experienced it. **You're the user/victim.**</mark>

- how did you find out?
- how long were you in the dark?
- what was the impact on you?

*Build a personal, emotional hook for why monitoring matters.*

<mark>**Day 2**: A system failed and you were part of the response. **You're the responder.**</mark>

- who got called?
- how was it escalated?
- what was the process? 

*Surface what incident response actually looks like in practice.*
